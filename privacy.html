Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

What we access

With your consent via TikTok Login Kit, the tool reads only:

  • Your basic profile (display name, avatar)
  • Your profile stats (followers, likes, video count)
  • Your public videos and their performance metrics

Where it lives

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the machine where you run the tool. Data is fetched from TikTok’s API and displayed to you only. Nothing is uploaded to third-party servers.

Revoking access

You can disconnect at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.
